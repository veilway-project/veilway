## How to sign

When you open your first pull request at https://github.com/veilway-project/veilway, the CLA bot posts a comment with a link to this document and the exact line to reply with. Replying with that line from your GitHub account is your signature, and your reply comment is the signature record. The bot does not ask again for later pull requests. If this document changes in substance, the version number changes and the bot asks for a new signature; earlier signatures keep covering earlier Contributions. A pull request cannot be merged until everyone with commits in it has signed.

If you contribute as part of your job, check section 7 before you sign.
